## PART 1 · Facts that come from one source, for every school

These are uniform, checkable and fast. **My School** (acara.edu.au) publishes all of them in the same format for every Australian school, which makes them the only genuinely comparable numbers we have.

| Field | Why the quiz needs it | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Enrolment **Years 7–12** | `schoolSize` — child said whether they want a big or small place | ✅ |
| **ICSEA** value + % in top quarter | `peerAmbition` — currently guessed, this measures it | ❌ |
| **Teaching staff FTE** | With enrolment, gives students-per-teacher. Best available proxy for `visibility` and `teacher` | ❌ |
| Non-teaching staff FTE | Support capacity | ❌ |
| Latitude / longitude | Distance from family suburb | ⚠️ 21/22 |

> John XXIII returned 131 teaching staff / 116.1 FTE / 1,492 students → **12.9 students per teaching FTE**. That single number is more defensible than any culture rating we currently hold, and it exists for all 22 schools on one page each.

**Fees — the headline figure is not the cost.** Record separately, never combined:

| Field | Status |
|---|---|
| Published Year 7 tuition + year | ⚠️ understated by 25–32% where checked |
| Capital works / building levy | ❌ |
| Resource or IT fee | ❌ |
| Compulsory family levies (P&F, magazine) | ❌ |
| One-off entry costs (application, enrolment, endowment) | ❌ |

> Hale's real first year is **$44,900** against $34,000 in the tool. John XXIII adds **$4,776** in compulsory extras to a $15,914 tuition. Fees are a hard filter, so understating them makes schools appear that a family cannot afford — and hides ones they can.

## PART 2 · Interests — what the child actually picked

The quiz opens by asking a child to choose three things they'd spend a Saturday doing. **Sixteen tiles. Only three have program data behind them.**

### Held

| Domain | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Sport** | Competition body (PSA / IGSSA / ACC / JTC / public) + 17 sports on a 2/1/0 scale | ✅ |
| **Music** | 9 sub-domains: orchestral, band, choral, jazz, contemporary, guitar, tuition, production, selective entry | ✅ |

### Missing — and children pick these

| Domain | Tiles it serves | What to find | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual art & design** | "Drawing, design or animation" | Art beyond compulsory years, design/graphics/textiles subjects, studios, exhibitions, portfolio entry | ❌ |
| **Drama, film, dance** | "Acting, film or performing" | Drama as a subject, production seasons, theatre space, media/screen subjects, dance | ❌ |
| **Computing & making** | "Coding, games or robotics", "Building or fixing things" | CS/IT subjects to Year 12, robotics team or competition entry, engineering, design & technology workshops, makerspace | ❌ |
| **Languages** | "Languages, travel or other countries" | Which languages, to what year, exchange or immersion programs | ❌ |
| **Service** | "Helping or coaching younger kids" | Structured service program, peer mentoring, whether it's compulsory and in which years | ❌ |
| **Outdoor** | "Being outdoors — camping, hiking" | Camp program by year, Duke of Edinburgh, outdoor education as a subject | ❌ |
| **Science & environment** | "Science experiments", "Looking after animals" | Science extension, competitions, farm or environmental program | ❌ |
| **Writing & media** | "Writing stories, or making videos" | Journalism, publications, media production, debating | ❌ |

**Computing and art are the two urgent ones.** Both `tech` and the art tile are already being scored — against attribute numbers with no program evidence underneath. A child who codes is currently matched on a guess.

Use the same three-state scale throughout:

> **2** = specialist program, selective or audition entry, dedicated facility, or documented competition record · **1** = offered as a subject or club · **0** = searched the subject list, not there

`0` and *blank* are different. A school that demonstrably has no dance program is a finding. A school nobody checked is not.

**Source:** the school's own subject list and co-curricular pages. This is the one place a school's website is the right source — a subject list is a fact, not a claim.

## PART 3 · Teaching, pedagogy and staff

This is where the tool is weakest and where the bias lives.

| Field | Where it exists | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **WA Public School Review** — quoted on teaching, support, leadership, areas for improvement | 8 public schools only. No equivalent exists for private schools anywhere in WA | ✅ 8/8 |
| **Staff reviews** — SEEK, Glassdoor, Indeed, teaching roles, 2023–2026, quoted and dated | The only corroboration available for private schools | ✅ 13/13 searched |
| **Published teaching framework** | School's own site — an instructional framework, coaching model, or explicit approach | ❌ |
| **Streaming / setting** | Which subjects, from which year, how placement is decided | ❌ |
| **Extension & support structures** | Named programs for both ends | ⚠️ |
| Current vacancies + any role advertised twice in 24 months | SEEK / school careers page | ⚠️ |

**Rules that matter here:**

- Quote the words. A rating with no text is nearly useless.
- **Report contradictions rather than averaging them.** All Saints has *"amazing culture"* and *"very high pressure… fear of parent complaints"* in the same year. Both are true for somebody.
- Teaching roles only. A 2019 groundskeeper review says nothing about how teachers are treated.
- **Finding nothing is a result.** Nine schools have no usable staff evidence and recording that plainly is as valuable as finding something.

## PART 4 · Pastoral structure — countable only

Discard adjectives. *"We care deeply about every student"* is on every website in Australia. Five questions:

1. **What is the pastoral unit called?** House, Tutor Group, Homeroom, Mentor Group
2. **How many students in one?** Published number only — never estimate
3. **How often does it meet?** Daily, weekly, as stated
4. **Does the same adult stay with a student across years?** `yes` / `no` / `not stated`. `no` if contact hands over at a year boundary. **Warm language is not a yes.**
5. **What is the published escalation chain?** e.g. "Tutor → Head of House → psychologist"

**Every non-blank answer needs the quoted sentence and the URL.** No sentence means `not stated`.

Question 4 is the most valuable and the most likely to be answered from vibe — every school implies continuous relationships, almost none state it. John XXIII was honest about this: House continuity across year levels, but *"the College does not publicly state that the same tutor, mentor or pastoral teacher follows a student across several years."*

Status: ❌ for 21 schools, ⚠️ for John XXIII, Aquinas, Carey, Fremantle College.

## PART 5 · Culture signals

| Field | Status |
|---|---|
| **Newsletter mix** — a full year, counting *editions mentioning* each of sport / music / arts / academic / service / leadership | ⚠️ 3 schools |
| **Community themes** — Reddit, Whirlpool, Facebook | ✅ searched, almost nothing meets the bar |

Two warnings on newsletters. It measures **what a school chooses to celebrate, not what students do** — a communications signal, not participation. And it is structurally biased: Applecross published 15 auditable editions, John XXIII 40, Aquinas 2, Carey **0** because theirs sit behind a parent portal. A blank is not evidence of absence.

Community threshold stays: **three independent accounts, 2023 or later, or it isn't a theme.**

## Priority, if you only do some of it

1. **My School figures** — enrolment, ICSEA, staff FTE. One page per school, uniform, and it replaces two guessed attributes with measurements.
2. **Computing and art programs** — both already scored against nothing.
3. **Real fee totals** — a hard filter currently wrong by up to a third.
4. **Pastoral question 4** — the one thing My School can't tell us.
5. Everything else.

## Watch for

Interstate and overseas namesakes derail these searches constantly. **Wesley College** (Melbourne), **Christ Church Grammar**, **Santa Maria**, **Aquinas** (a US institution dominates results), **Seton** (US), **Corpus Christi** (Canada), **John XXIII** (the ANU residential college), **Carey** (the Victorian grammar school — and our campus is *Harrisdale*, not Forrestdale). Confirm Perth, Western Australia before recording anything.
