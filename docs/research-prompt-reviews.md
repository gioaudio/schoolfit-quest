# Find three WA Public School Review reports and quote them

Do the research now. Return the quotes. No plan, no scope, no methodology section, no summary of what you intend to do.

There is **one task**. Nothing else in this request matters.

## The task

Find the **WA Department of Education Public School Review** report for each of these three schools:

1. **Perth Modern School**
2. **John Curtin College of the Arts**
3. **Rossmoyne Senior High School**

All three are Western Australian government schools. The Department publishes an external review report for every public school. The reports are PDFs, usually reachable from the school's own website or from education.wa.edu.au. They exist. Find them.

## What to extract

For each school, quote **the actual sentences from the report** — not a paraphrase, not a summary, not your characterisation of the tone. Copy the words.

Cover these four topics:

- **Teaching quality** — what the reviewers said about instruction, pedagogy, teacher practice
- **Student support** — pastoral care, wellbeing, student services, intervention for students at risk
- **Leadership** — school leadership, governance, distributed leadership, planning
- **Areas for improvement** — the recommendations, and anything the reviewers identified as a weakness

Give the **review year**, the **page number** for each quote, and the **URL** of the report.

If the report genuinely does not address one of the four topics, write "not found" for that topic. Do not fill a gap with something approximate.

## Format

```
### Perth Modern School
Review year:
Report URL:

Teaching quality: "…" (p.X)
Student support: "…" (p.X)
Leadership: "…" (p.X)
Areas for improvement: "…" (p.X)
```

Repeat for John Curtin College of the Arts and Rossmoyne Senior High School.

## Rules

- Quote verbatim. If you are rewording, you are doing it wrong.
- Include the negative findings. The recommendations and identified weaknesses are the most valuable part of the report — a review that only produced praise has been read selectively.
- No commentary, no interpretation, no ranking of the three schools against each other.
- If you cannot locate a report after genuine searching, say so plainly and name where you looked. Do not substitute the school's annual report, its own website, or a news article.

Start with Perth Modern School.
