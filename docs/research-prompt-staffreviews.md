# Find staff reviews for nine Perth private schools

Do the research now. Return the reviews. No plan, no scope, no methodology section.

There is **one task**. Nothing else in this request matters.

## The task

For each school below, search **SEEK**, **Glassdoor** and **Indeed** for employee reviews, and report what current and former **teaching staff** actually wrote.

1. All Saints' College, Bull Creek
2. Corpus Christi College, Bateman
3. Kennedy Baptist College, Murdoch
4. Christ Church Grammar School, Claremont
5. Wesley College, South Perth
6. CBC Fremantle
7. Iona Presentation College, Mosman Park
8. Santa Maria College, Attadale
9. Seton Catholic College, Samson

All are in Perth, Western Australia. Several have similarly named schools in other states — **Wesley College** exists in Melbourne, **Christ Church Grammar** and **Santa Maria** have interstate namesakes, and **Aquinas** searches return a US institution. Confirm you are reading the Perth school before recording anything.

## What counts

- **Quote the words.** A rating without text is nearly useless. What people wrote is the evidence.
- **Teaching roles are what matter.** A 2019 review from a groundskeeper tells us nothing about how the school treats teachers. Record that it exists, then mark it unusable.
- **2023 onward is evidence. Earlier is background.** Say which is which.
- **Report both directions.** If reviews contradict each other, that is the finding — quote both sides rather than averaging them into nothing. Do not select the reviews that agree with each other.
- **Do not add review counts across platforms.** The same person may post twice.

## Silence is the point

Several of these schools will have no usable staff reviews at all. **That is a real and wanted result**, not a failure. Say so explicitly:

> "Searched SEEK, Glassdoor and Indeed. N reviews total, none from teaching staff, none after YYYY. No usable staff evidence exists for this school."

This tool currently rates some of these schools on their own marketing material alone. Confirming that nothing independent exists is exactly as valuable as finding something.
